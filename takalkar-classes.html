Who We Are

About Takalkar Classes

Takalkar Classes is Hadapsar's most trusted coaching institute, established with a mission to provide quality, accessible education to every student in the community.

Located conveniently in Shivam Complex above Sadhana Sahakari Bank, Gadital, we serve students across SSC, HSC & CBSE boards with personalised attention that large schools simply can't provide.

  • Small batch sizes ensuring individual attention for every student
  • Regular tests, mock exams & performance tracking
  • Doubt-clearing sessions 6 days a week
  • Parent-teacher meetings & progress reports every term
  • Study material designed by experienced faculty
  • Positive, motivating learning environment
